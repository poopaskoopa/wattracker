# Just Ride variety

Just Ride variants change the shape of a session while keeping its training
purpose recognizable. The picker should describe the target power curve, not
promise that two shapes are physiologically identical for every rider.

## Guardrails

- Endurance and recovery variants stay in the low-intensity range and use
  pacing changes, gentle progressions, or cadence prompts rather than adding
  hidden hard efforts.
- Tempo variants stay in Zone 3. Threshold variants can use controlled
  over-unders because the brief over-threshold work is the intended threshold
  stimulus, not an accidental sprint.
- VO2 variants vary interval duration and power while remaining in the
  high-aerobic interval domain. Sprint sessions remain short maximal efforts
  with substantial recovery and no ERG power target.
- Variant duration, estimated load, and target ranges are checked in tests;
  the classic shape remains the default for old links and stored workouts.
