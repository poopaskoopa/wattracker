The head is server-rendered because none of it depends on the data: the tiles quote a period ("vs prev 4") the page otherwise never names, and nothing about a row of cards says the cards are controls. The comparison clause is a span rather than plain text because it is the one half of the note that can be false: with no earlier four-week window, or one that logged nothing, every tile reads "no prior"/"no baseline" and no comparison happened. volume.js hides the span in that case, which leaves the remaining sentence grammatical on its own. #}
